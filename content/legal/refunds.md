# Refund and cancellation policy

Draft · last updated 23 November 2026

## Cancelling Pro

You can cancel at any time in **Settings → Cancel plan**. Cancelling stops the next renewal. Pro stays active until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

## Refunds

Payments for Pro, monthly or yearly, are **not refundable**, including for unused time in the current period.

We refund a charge in full if:

- you were charged twice for the same period;
- you were charged after you cancelled; or
- you were charged the wrong amount.

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} within 7 days of the charge. Include your account email and the Razorpay payment ID from your receipt. If we confirm the error, we refund it to your original payment method through Razorpay. Banks usually take 5–7 working days to show it.

## Free plan

The free plan never charges you.
