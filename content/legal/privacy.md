# Privacy policy

Draft · last updated 23 November 2026

StudyRaven ({{SITE_URL}}) is run by {{OPERATOR}}, {{OPERATOR_CITY}}, India ("we"). This page explains what we collect, why, who helps us process it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Questions: {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}.

## The short version

- We collect what we need to run your study tools, and nothing more.
- No ads, no selling your data, no advertising or analytics trackers.
- Text you send to an AI feature goes to an AI provider only to produce your result.
- You can ask us to show, correct or delete your data at any time.

## Students under 18

Most StudyRaven users are under 18. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, a person under 18 is a child, and their data needs a parent or guardian's consent. If you are under 18, use StudyRaven only with your parent or guardian's agreement. We are building a step where a parent confirms consent by email. Until then, a parent or guardian can email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}} at any time to see or delete their child's data.

## What we collect and why

| Data | Why |
|---|---|
| Name, email, and a password (stored hashed by Supabase) or your Google sign-in | To create your account and log you in |
| Grade, curriculum, country, subjects, exam dates, target grades | To personalise study plans, papers and countdowns |
| What you create: tasks, notes, flashcards, study kits, PDFs you upload, mock answers and results, mistakes, drills, topic ratings | To show your work back to you and track progress |
| College guide (Pro): target countries and universities, interests, activities, application tracker, chat messages | To build your college plan and keep your chat history |
| Essay drafts and AI tutor messages | Sent to the AI to give feedback or an answer; not saved by us afterwards |
| Gmail / Classroom (optional beta, only if you connect them): email subject lines, coursework titles and due dates, and a Google access token | To import deadlines. You can disconnect at any time |
| Pro payments: Razorpay subscription ID, plan, billing cycle, renewal date | To run your subscription. Razorpay handles card and UPI details; we never see them |
| Usage: daily AI generation count, when a mock was started, email preferences | To apply free-plan limits and send the reminders you asked for |

**Cookies:** only the login cookies needed to keep you signed in, and your light/dark theme choice saved in your browser. No advertising or analytics cookies.

## Emails

We send account emails (sign-in, password reset), mock results, reminders about deadlines, exams and unfinished mocks, a weekly digest, and occasional emails about Pro. You can turn off non-essential emails in Settings.

## How long we keep data

We keep your data while your account is open. If you ask us to delete your account, we delete it and the data linked to it, including uploaded files, within 30 days. Razorpay keeps payment records for as long as the law requires.

## Your rights

You (or your parent or guardian, if you are under 18) can ask us to:

- show you the personal data we hold about you;
- correct or update it;
- delete it, or withdraw consent;
- name someone to act for you if you are unable to.

Email {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We aim to reply within 7 days. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

## Security

Each account can only read its own data (enforced in the database), uploaded files are stored privately, and all traffic uses HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; if something goes wrong that affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
